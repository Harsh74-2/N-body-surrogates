Interactive trajectory viewer. Drag the slider to step through the rollout; the dashed curve is the closed-form Kepler reference, the solid curve is the surrogate prediction. Hover the bodies for frame coordinates (AU).

Solid coloured = surrogate prediction (GNN). Dashed grey = closed-form reference. Slider steps through frames 0..114 (each frame = dt step). Per-body colors and traces are loaded from the embedded JSON.
