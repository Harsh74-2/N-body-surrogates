N=50 — Inner Planets — GNN

Solid coloured markers = surrogate prediction (GNN); open grey markers = closed-form reference; dashed grey lines = full reference paths; faint solid lines = full predicted paths. Each frame is one rollout step (dt = yr). Positions are primary-centred, in AU. Hover a body for coordinates. Drag the slider to scrub; buttons play/pause; the select sets playback speed. A pre-rendered mp4 of the same rollout is also available.
