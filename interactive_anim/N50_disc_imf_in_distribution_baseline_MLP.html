N=50 — Disc Imf In Distribution Baseline — MLP

Solid coloured markers = surrogate prediction (MLP); open grey markers = closed-form reference; dashed grey lines = full reference paths; faint solid lines = full predicted paths. Each frame is one rollout step (dt = yr). This dataset is unit-normalised (code units, no AU scaling) and has no central primary, so positions are shown as stored. Hover a body for coordinates. Drag the slider to scrub; buttons play/pause; the select sets playback speed. A pre-rendered mp4 of the same rollout is also available.
